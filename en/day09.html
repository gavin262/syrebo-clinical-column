Issue 09

Faradic Stimulation Combined with Syrebo C12: A 4-Week Record of One Patient with Left Hemiparesis

From the paper

Rehabilitation of Left-Sided Hemiparesis Post-MCA Infarction Using Faradic Electrical Stimulation and Robotic Hand Rehabilitation: A Case Report

Prabhu C, Mohan P, Sajjana D

International Journal of Physiotherapy Research and Clinical Practice, 2025, 4(2): 27-30

IJPRCP, 2025, 4(2): 27-30

View source

Syrebo C12 powered glove and controller (cropped from the official C12 product page photo on syrebocare.com)

Post-stroke hemiparesis rehabilitation sometimes stacks two approaches: electrical stimulation to activate muscles, and a robotic hand to move the fingers. This case report documents one such combined intervention in full.

The case

The patient, a 60-year-old man with left-sided hemiparesis, began intervention 3 months and 2 weeks after stroke. The protocol combined faradic electrical stimulation with Syrebo C12 robotic hand rehabilitation, for 4 weeks at 5 sessions per week.

What this case shows

The value of a case report lies in documenting a combined intervention as actually delivered: how electrical stimulation and robotic hand training were used together, under professional guidance, in one hemiparetic patient's daily rehabilitation. For clinicians and families considering a similar combined approach, this record offers a genuine reference point.

Single-case combined intervention; changes cannot be attributed to one device alone, and results are not generalizable.

Source: Prabhu C, Mohan P, Sajjana D. Rehabilitation of Left-Sided Hemiparesis Post-MCA Infarction Using Faradic Electrical Stimulation and Robotic Hand Rehabilitation: A Case Report. International Journal of Physiotherapy Research and Clinical Practice, 2025, 4(2): 27-30. Source page
