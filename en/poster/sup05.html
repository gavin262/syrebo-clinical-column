Supplement 05 · Image version

A soft glove and virtual scenes: brain activation in 20 healthy adults and 20 patients, not an efficacy trial

Source: P. Liu, X. Yang, F. Han, G. Peng, Q. Li, L. Huang, L. Wang, Y. Fan. Brain Activation Pattern Caused by Soft Rehabilitation Glove and Virtual Reality Scenes: A Pilot fNIRS Study. IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems and Rehabilitation Engineering, 2024;32:3848–3857. DOI: 10.1109/TNSRE.2024.3482470.
Limits: A pilot mechanism study. Clinical recovery was not the primary outcome. Activation differences can reflect compensation and should not be read as “higher is better.” Brand and model remain unchecked, so this is not a confirmed device experiment.
Note: Rehabilitation outcomes vary by individual. Please follow the training plan prescribed by your physician or therapist.
