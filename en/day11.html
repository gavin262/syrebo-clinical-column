Home Rehabilitation, Recognized in Japan: Syrebo Named Among the Few Commercialized Home-Use Finger Robots

Home rehabilitation has been talked about for years, but products that actually reach patients' homes and get used every day remain rare. A 2023 article in the Journal of the Robotics Society of Japan (Vol 41, No 4), on home-use finger rehabilitation robots for hemiplegia and collaboration with medical professionals, made exactly this point: while commercialized cases are still few, products realized for home use do exist — and the article names the Syrebo® hand rehabilitation glove as one of them.

The Journal of the Robotics Society of Japan (2023) cited the Syrebo® hand rehabilitation glove as one of the few hand rehabilitation robots worldwide successfully commercialized for home use. Home rehabilitation is not just a concept — Syrebo has already done it. What makes that possible is the same route behind the rest of this column: a soft, wearable design that patients can actually use at home, extending rehabilitation from the hospital into daily life.

Study limitations: This is a mention in a review article, not a clinical trial of Syrebo; the article itself stresses that such commercialized cases remain few.
