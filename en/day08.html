Issue 08

Innovative Mirror Therapy: Preliminary Results from a 60-Patient Randomized Study

From the paper

创新式镜像疗法对脑卒中患者上肢功能障碍的康复效果

陈涛、刘沙鑫、陈雪莲、毛磊、李德波、甘林玉

新医学, 2024, 55(6): 397-402

DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.0253-9802.2024.06.001

View source

SY-HR08E mirror training scene with screen, host and gloves (official photo)

Mirror therapy is a classic approach in post-stroke upper limb rehabilitation. "Innovative" mirror therapy combines mirror training with a rehabilitation robotic glove, guiding the affected hand through more standardized training under mirror guidance. A 60-patient randomized study compared this approach with traditional mirror therapy.

How the study was done

Sixty patients with post-stroke upper limb dysfunction were randomly assigned using a random number table, 30 per group, for a 4-week intervention: an innovative mirror therapy group versus a traditional mirror therapy group.

What it found

The innovative mirror therapy group showed greater improvements than the traditional mirror therapy group on FMA-UE (upper extremity motor function), FMA-WH (wrist-hand function), and the Modified Barthel Index (daily living ability). On brand attribution, the training images in the original journal pages show the Yisheng logo (Syrebo’s Chinese brand name), corresponding to the SY-HR08E in official materials.
