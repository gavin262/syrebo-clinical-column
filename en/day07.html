After a Middle Cerebral Artery Infarct: One 66-Year-Old Patient's Syrebo Glove Rehabilitation

Generic soft rehabilitation glove illustration; the source does not specify a model, so no model correspondence is claimed

Sometimes a complete rehabilitation story says more than cold numbers. This case report documents the full course of a 66-year-old woman who underwent Syrebo glove rehabilitation after an infarct in the middle cerebral artery territory.

The case

The patient, a 66-year-old woman, was diagnosed with bilateral middle cerebral artery territory infarct. After stabilization, she received neurophysiotherapy centered on the Syrebo glove rehabilitation program, and the report records her observed progress and functional changes during rehabilitation.

What this case shows

The value of a case report lies in showing what happened in one person: how a soft robotic glove was used, under professional guidance, in the daily rehabilitation of a patient after a severe cerebral infarct. For patients and families facing similar difficulties, such a record offers a genuine reference point.

Single-case report; not generalizable.

Source: Sharma VS, Sharath HV, Sasun AR. Effectiveness of Syrebo's Glove Rehabilitation Program in a Patient With Middle Cerebral Artery Infarct: A Case Report. Cureus, 2024, 16(4): e59314. DOI: 10.7759/cureus.59314 (PMID: 38817453).
